Understanding 1/2

Think of a fraction as a "part" of a whole. Imagine we have a big, delicious pepperoni pizza!

1

The Denominator (bottom number) tells us how many total equal slices the pizza is cut into. In 1/2, that's 2 slices!

2

The Numerator (top number) tells us how many of those slices you get to eat. In 1/2, you get 1 slice!

lightbulb

Key Rule

The larger the denominator, the smaller each individual slice becomes!

person

That makes sense! So if it was 1/4, the slices would be smaller than 1/2?

smart_toy

Exactly! You're a natural at this. 🍕 If you share with 4 people, everyone gets a smaller piece than if you only share with 2.
